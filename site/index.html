How accurate is this?
  • The percentage is the model's chance that the player lands on your side of the line. Change the line to the one your bookmaker offers and it recalculates.
  • Tested on past games the model had not seen: when it said 70–80% the prop hit about 73% of the time. Above 90% it hit between 86% and 94%, depending on the stat, so this page never shows more than 90%.
  • Edge is the expected profit for every 100 you stake at the odds you typed. A positive number means the odds pay more than the model thinks the bet is worth. It is an estimate, not a promise.
  • Not covered: the effect of a teammate being out (flagged on the card, not priced in). Top scorers outscored the model by about 0.7 points in testing. Real bookmaker lines have not been back-tested. Only one and a half seasons of games are in the data.
  • This only suggests bets; it places nothing. Check what is legal where you are, and only stake money you can afford to lose.
