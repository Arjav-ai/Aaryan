How to use this

  1. Open a match and find the player or bet you like.
  2. Type the line and odds your bookmaker shows next to it.
  3. Read the verdict. Green means the odds pay more than the model thinks the bet is worth. Red means skip it.

These are estimates from past games. They have not been shown to beat the bookmaker, so treat them as a second opinion, not a promise.
